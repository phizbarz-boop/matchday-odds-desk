Both website and Telegram analyzers now require one unambiguous match to the original event, market, outcome and line. A match ID, similar price or another selection on the ticket cannot establish a bet type. Handicap signs and line specifiers are preserved. Unknown/generic selections stay unsupported rather than becoming Over 1.5. Booked labels and odds remain on the analysis; current odds are separate. Replacement still requires the explicit Replace Unsupported action.
