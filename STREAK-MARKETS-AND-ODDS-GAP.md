New Football bet types: Any Team To Score 2 or More Goals in a Row Yes/No (SportyBet 60010, outcomes 74/76) and 3 or More Yes/No (60020). Both website and Telegram builder expose the four choices. Consecutive goal order is modeled; opposition scoring resets the run. Existing displayed-statistics Poisson rates take priority, with a labelled ordinary-market-fitted Poisson fallback. If a goal-rate model cannot be established, a complete two-sided Yes/No price set can supply a labelled no-vig market estimate. Missing or inconsistent inputs are not independent predictions. Results require official goal-order settlement; full-time score alone is never used to infer a streak.

Optional Win odds gap >5.00 filter is OFF by default. It compares the absolute difference between the two opponents’ regular winner decimal odds in the SAME fixture and live/prematch state, never odds across different games, draw odds, total-goal odds or a ratio. Both prices must be current and usable. A gap exactly equal to 5.00 fails. Winner price feeds are loaded even when another bet type is selected. Raw fixture rows are filtered before the shared probability model is applied, for website and Telegram builders; live rows are rechecked after live collection and before live modeling. Expanded detailed markets must also pass a fresh event winner-price comparison. Existing probability floors and red-flag checks then apply. Missing counterpart prices exclude the match when the option is ON.

Website preference is saved locally and retained when regenerating. Telegram preference is saved with builder settings and appears as a toggle. Existing membership permissions still apply. Scheduled Telegram batches remain on their existing settings; this option does not silently alter scheduled plans. These are model estimates, not empirically established predictive accuracy or guaranteed wins.
