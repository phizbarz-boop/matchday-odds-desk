Website football markets and report update

Adds Away Team Offsides Over 0.5, Home Team Offsides Over 0.5, and Total Goals 1–5 Minutes Under 0.5 to website market browsing and Auto Builder. Discovery uses the public fixture/event feed, retaining exact supplied market IDs, outcome IDs, specifiers and offered odds. No unobserved market ID is hardcoded. Only matching labels, period and line are accepted. Modelled probabilities use a complete current two-outcome market with the margin removed, labelled as market estimates rather than independent historical predictions. Missing opposite prices or unavailable markets are not scored. These specialties require official settlement; final-match goals cannot settle offsides or the five-minute market. They are not assigned full-match goal H2H support.

Auto Builder now has its own searchable All leagues / Selected leagues only selector, with multiple choices and saved preferences. Options come from the existing public multi-sport catalogue; readiness depends on that catalogue. Server final filtering enforces tournament names for all sports; empty selection returns no games. Regeneration retains the original request’s leagues. Main football browsing chips remain separate.

Telegram 12-hour report is bounded to 1,800 characters and contains counts plus hypothetical ₦100 stake returns, net P/L, settled/unresolved stakes and ROI. It includes a brief data-qualified recommendation but no match detail, team names, individual code lists or multi-message leg list. Full records remain stored for calculations. Reviewed game counts include newly updated older tickets and count repeated legs per ticket.

No live bookings or Telegram messages were sent in testing.
