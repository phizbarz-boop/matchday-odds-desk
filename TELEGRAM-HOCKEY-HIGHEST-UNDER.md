Ice hockey daily long shot

Adds one daily ice-hockey ticket at 07:00 and 18:00 WAT, using the existing model and a 70% minimum probability, up to 20 distinct fixtures. It accepts match-goals Under 6.5, 7.5 or 8.5 only when that is the highest active Under offered in the same exact match-goals selector. Highest is determined from source rows before the probability and range filters. A higher offered 9.5 line means lower 6.5–8.5 lines do not qualify. If the highest line fails the probability floor, the app does not substitute a lower line.

Final public event validation recomputes eligibility from fresh market rows before booking. Player/team/period totals, Over, handicaps and shots are excluded. Exact SportyBet market/outcome/specifier and offered odds are retained. Up to 20 permits a shorter ticket when fewer eligible fixtures exist. Other long shots continue to exclude totals. Combo tickets remain 60%. Existing current-session Today’s Codes storage and report tracking apply.

No live booking or Telegram send was performed during tests.
