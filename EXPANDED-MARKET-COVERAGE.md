# Expanded SportyBet market coverage

This update expands the original fixed 50 selection categories. It does not claim complete modeling of every SportyBet market or every sport.

The website Auto Builder has a checked 'Include additional supported SportyBet prematch markets' option. Turning it off restores the selected fixed categories only. Fresh prematch collection scans full event detail market lists across the selected supported sports. Scheduled next-12h and three-hourly jobs with unrestricted bet types also use expanded discovery. The morning SAFE batch retains its curated categories. Live/QC retains the existing settlement/progress-aware live model; new prematch categories are not automatically treated as currently winning live picks.

Additional modeling applies to complete Yes/No, Odd/Even, two-way winner, three-way result and half-line Over/Under groups. It supports arbitrary market IDs, period/team/player scopes, and lines when the complete group is available. Complete nine-way Half Time/Full Time, exact-count partitions with a final N+ bucket, and correct-score sets with an explicit 'other score' remainder are also supported. A single incomplete price or an inactive missing outcome cannot be normalized into a high probability. Existing statistical model probabilities take precedence for already modeled selections.

The fallback removes bookmaker margin from a complete set of mutually exclusive prices, just as the app's existing other-sport models do. This is a market-derived probability estimate, not an independently fitted player/team performance model. It is labelled 'offered_prices' and cannot be represented as evidence of bookmaker mispricing. Its quality score is probability-only; no empirical reliability is claimed. Unknown partitions, overlaps, integer/quarter push lines and early-payout/insurance markets remain explicitly unmodeled. Models for these require settlement-aware probabilities or additional statistics.

GET /api/sportybet/market-coverage reports discovered market instances, extra market estimates, unsupported reasons and outcome counts from the latest public catalogue. Auto-pick responses also include collection and coverage diagnostics. An absent or failed full-detail read cannot imply exhaustive coverage. Fixture pagination limits and read errors are reported. The existing six supported sports are Football, Basketball, Ice Hockey, Tennis, Handball and Volleyball. Other sports, virtuals and casino products are not included in this update.

Set SPORTYBET_EXPANDED_MARKETS_ENABLED=false to disable expanded on-demand scans. Expanded collection can take longer than the original fixed-family requests. Public read access remains separate from booking, and unsupported models are not filled with fabricated probabilities. Official SportyBet settlement can still resolve a posted specialty selection; fallback local settlement remains pending where the evaluator does not understand a specialty market.

Install this package over the prior project and push to trigger your normal deployment. This package is an expansion and coverage audit, not a completed universal model. Remaining work includes discovery/modeling of additional sports, custom settlement models for non-binary handicaps and promotions, player statistics and specialty live eligibility.
