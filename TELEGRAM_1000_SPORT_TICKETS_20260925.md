# Telegram 1000 Sport Tickets — 2026-09-25

Added three scheduled Telegram Auto Pick tickets without changing the existing SAFE, 2x, or 3x rules:

- **1000 ICE HOCKEY** — Ice Hockey only
- **1000 BASKETBALL** — Basketball only
- **1000 HANDBALL + VOLLEYBALL** — Handball and Volleyball combined

Rules for all three new tickets:

- Minimum probability per selection: **80%**
- Maximum selections: **15**
- Target combined odds: **1000x**
- 1000x is a **flexible target**. If today's qualifying pool cannot reach 1000x, the best available qualifying ticket is still generated and posted with its actual combined odds.
- Match-winner selections are tried before totals/handicaps/other supported markets.
- Red-flag protection remains enabled.
- One selection per fixture is retained.
- Existing SAFE 1.30–5.00 (85%), 2x (80%), and 3x (80%) tickets are unchanged.
