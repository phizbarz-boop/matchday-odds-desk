# Telegram Daily Auto Picks: 50x / 100x and winner-first high odds

- 10x, 20x, 50x and 100x: match-winner only, >=80% estimated probability per selection.
- 1000x and 10000x: try winner-only first; if the target is unreachable, keep winners and supplement with other currently supported markets on different matches. All legs remain >=80% estimated probability.
- 10000x explicitly scans Football, Basketball, Ice Hockey, Handball, Volleyball and Tennis, regardless of TELEGRAM_SPORT_SCOPE. Eligibility still depends on available same-day fixtures.
- SAFE remains winner-only, >=90% and combined odds 1.30-5.00.
- Telegram tickets are capped at 40 selections, with one selection per fixture and red-flag protection.
- No markets removed in earlier releases (1UP and first-half team corners) are restored.
- The site Auto Builder and interactive Telegram AI Builder remain unchanged.
- Generated-code listing and /api/telegram/status include 50x and 100x.
- Combined odds are targets, not guarantees; the bot still labels a closest-available combination accurately when a target cannot be reached.
