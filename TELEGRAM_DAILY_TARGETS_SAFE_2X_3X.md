# Scheduled Telegram Auto Picks — SAFE, 2x and 3x

The once-daily Telegram Auto Pick at 08:25 WAT generates **only**:

- SAFE (combined odds 1.30–5.00; each selection model probability ≥90%).
- 2x (combined odds at least 2.00; each selection model probability ≥90%).
- 3x (combined odds at least 3.00; each selection model probability ≥90%).

All three use supported markets (including Over/Under), with Ice Hockey and Basketball priority, followed by Handball, Volleyball, Tennis and Football as needed. The existing red-flag checks, one leg per match, maximum 40 legs and no positive-edge requirement remain. SAFE enforces its 1.30–5.00 range; 2x and 3x do not publish a ticket that falls short of the corresponding target.

The scheduled 10x, 20x, 1000x and 10000x sets were removed from the daily Telegram run, daily header, Today's Codes view and Telegram status endpoint. These changes do not disable independently requested tickets in the interactive Telegram AI Builder or the website Auto Builder.

Free Today's Codes access is now SAFE + 2x (replacing SAFE + 10x); 3x requires Pro/Elite. Codes generated by earlier versions are not presented as current target tickets. No change to the workflow schedule, refreshes or subscription prices.

> Probabilities are model estimates, not guarantees. Live bookmaker availability and remote deployment have not been tested.
